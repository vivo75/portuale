## Pattern translation

Real compiles the PATTERN with Python `re`. The port translates it to
`regex::bytes` with `(?-u)` (ASCII `\w`/`\s`, byte `.`), `\A`, and
`\n?\z` for a trailing `$`. Some tokens mean something different in
Rust syntax:
- nested or POSIX classes, such as `[[:alpha:]]` and `[a[b]c]`;
- the set operators `&&`, `--` and `~~` inside a class;
- `(?<name>`;
- `(?u)`.

The port refuses those with rc 1 and an empty stdout. It never
reinterprets them. No pattern that real Portage passes uses them: every
`captured-*` case passes.
