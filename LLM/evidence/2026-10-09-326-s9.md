Plan: `docs/02.326-no-portage-runtime.opus.md`, step S9.

clients. `3rdparty/portage` is now only:

## Changes

portuale, docs and comments only. They correct claims that the checkout
is needed at runtime:
- `docs/agent-context.md`:
  - the "Merge-path safety gate" paragraph: the gate runs with no
    checkout, and the #151 death is gone;
  - the `3rdparty/` paragraph: pmtest's `3rdparty/` is its own
    directory, not a symlink, and the gpg keyring lives in pmtest
    fixtures. The symlink claim predated #326; I corrected it in review.
- `bin/README.md`: "What's not here", and the re-sync rule that a new
  Python call site needs its helper ported first.
- `3rdparty/repos.toml` and `3rdparty/README.md`: the `[portage]`
  description, and the vendored `cnf/sets/portage.conf` note.
- Comments in:
  - `ebuild_phases.rs`: the `portage_checkout()` doc, the
    `PORTAGE_PYM_PATH` and `PYTHONPATH` docs, and the `phase_env_vars`
    comment;
  - `ebuild.rs` and `ebuild_package.rs`: the module comments;
  - `emerge_build.rs`: two test comments. The agent verified both tests
    pass with `PORTUALE_PORTAGE_CHECKOUT=/nonexistent`;
  - `remote_bundle.rs`: a stale span `:1072-1082` → `:1141-1153`.

pmtest:
- `run/lib.sh` `portuale_phase_helpers_preflight` requires the checkout
  only under `PORTUALE_PYTHON_HELPERS=real`.
- A new `PM_NO_CHECKOUT=1` mount mode. It mounts the repo, masks
  `3rdparty/portage` with an empty directory, and pins
  `PORTUALE_PORTAGE_CHECKOUT` to the masked path. Precedence is
  `PM_BARE` > `PM_NO_CHECKOUT` > `PM_RELOCATED`.
- A README section, "Container mount modes".
