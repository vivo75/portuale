`gpkg.tar_safe_extract.extractall` (`/usr/lib/python3.14/site-packages/portage/gpkg.py:662-768`;
root = the temp dir, `prefix="image"`), GNU tar is the 1.35 the merge path shells

Rules that fall out (all reproduced above):

- A symlink target containing `..` is extracted by Python `tarfile` (follows it)
  but deferred by GNU tar 1.35: any member under such a link fails with
  `Cannot open: Not a directory`, tar exits 2 after a partial unpack. Portage
  accepts the checks, GNU tar cannot do the extraction: the (b)-with-member
  and (c)-dotdot composed divergences.
- A data member after a hard link member breaks the *streaming* (zstd pipe)
  Portage extraction with `StreamError: seeking backwards is not allowed`
  (Q10); the same members over a seekable tar extract rc 0. The L5 cell pins
  the composed (1, 1) pair.
- Checks are all Portage-exact under the fixed root (parent of `image/`):
  `u-relocated-rel` names `image/../../marker` (was `image/opt/link`),
  `u-relocated-out-of-place` is accepted (was refused).
