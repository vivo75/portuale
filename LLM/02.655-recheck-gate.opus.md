**Goal:** Portuale holds the installed virtual bound on the real perl-5.44 tree everywhere Portage does — by deciding each committed seed against the post-skip graph instead of the commit-time graph, so fall-through arms that the skip demotes stay unmerged while genuine upgrades still merge.

**Architecture:** S0 #653 proved the misfire is NOT the re-pop (zero `SEED drop-stale` on both traced cells) but two live shapes that share one root defect — the seed verdict is latched at commit time and never re-examined: (a) dev-lang_perl commits seeds under a live demoter, the enforcement gate tolerates instead of enforcing, and no conflict is ever recorded, so the upgrade sails through where Portage heals and skips; (b) libreoffice records the conflict and the withhold fires, yet hold/commit assign inverted vs the bed oracle (TH merges, Encode drops). Candidate gates (Task 1 decides exactly one, oracle-arbitrated): (i) re-fire the downgrade guard against the post-skip graph at seed-consumption time (true late-eval: a seed whose arm no longer survives the guard against the skipped graph is dropped or demoted back to a pin); (ii) demoter-liveness check at seed time (drop seeds whose demoter is already masked) PLUS re-check of survivors (demoter masked later → void); (iii) re-resolution on skip-settle (re-run the consumer dep evaluation after a skip lands, limited to affected consumers). A third shape needs a controller ruling in the ledger, not a silent invention. The #650 keep-verdict proxy, #651 skip-if-demoter-masked, and #646 tie stay exactly as they are — the new gate covers what they provably miss (post-skip state), it does not replace them.
