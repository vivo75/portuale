- **(b)** Keep 3.0.82.2 and port only items that cannot differ on a
  non-hostile input. Those are rows 7-9, plus row 12 if accepted.

  rows 7-9;
- a bed image variant with `=sys-apps/portage-3.0.83-r1` for rows 2, 3, 10
