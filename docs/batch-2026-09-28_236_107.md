## 4. Slice A — probe-side upgrade forcing

**Goal (backlog #236, step 1).** An upgrade-forcing path in the resolver
loop, driven by the #211 slot-operator update probe, so the provider upgrade
and the consumer rebuilds can be re-created when they are wanted — before any
minimizer is allowed to collapse them away.

**Real source (start here, then read real).** `_slot_operator_update_probe`
(`_emerge/depgraph.py:2576`) and its backtracking companion
`_slot_operator_update_backtrack` (`:2400`) — the latter is the whole
mechanism: it masks the stale choice, records a `replace` set, and restarts
the resolve so the next pass selects the wanted upgrade. The probe's own
helpers are `_select_atoms_probe` (`:2960`) and
`_slot_operator_update_probe_slot_conflict` (`:2453`). The port's existing
post-pass scan is `slot_operator_rebuild_scan` (`portage-repo/src/lib.rs`);
the #211 work already ported the probe's new-child-slot arm and the
`check_reverse_dependencies` refusal. What is missing is the **forcing**: in
portuale the provider upgrade only comes from selection (`:=` selects the
highest tree candidate), so nothing re-creates an upgrade that a later
collapse would remove.

**What `g211-report.md` §5 established** (2026-09-28, worktree release
binary; background for S0, not a substitute for it):

- **E1 — the upgrade is selection-driven.** On the slotop argument shape
  (`-uDvN --oneshot dev-libs/consrdep`, ad-hoc ROOT, EAPI-faithful vdb)
  portuale merges `U provpkg-2.0 + rR consrdep-1.0`; `--debug` shows the
  live `provpkg:=` resolving to the merge 2.0 while the recorded
  `>=provpkg-1.0:0/1=` resolves to installed 1.0. Real binds installed and
  re-upgrades through the probe.
- **E2 — a literal minimizer kills it deterministically.** Real's
  elimination (installed-first) over {2.0 (`:=`), 1.0 (recorded)} drops
  2.0; with no `Upgrade` entry every post-pass scan (same-slot arm, R1,
  R2, item 2) sees nothing, and the **argument** cells (the 15 bed cells)
  go from `{U provpkg-2.0, rR…}` to `{}`. The `@world` cells survive
  (the provider's own world-seed atom is single-atom, minimizer-vacuous).
- **E3 — there is no collapse point.** `enqueue_dependencies` resolves
  through `use_reduce_flat_disjunctive` + per-atom
  `disjunction_preference`; (atom → pkg) pairs never meet as a per-parent
  set. Slice B builds that grouping; it is new selection architecture.
- **E4 — no in-scope shortcut.** Any selection rule that keeps the slotop
  upgrade while collapsing r25 is veto/`want_update` gating real does not
  have; 09-27 B13 rejected exactly that.

**Design choice (B8).** Real's shape is masks + `replace` set + restart.
S0 names the exact decision point in portuale's loop where real calls
`_slot_operator_update_backtrack`, and the S1 port follows that shape unless
the probe proves an equivalent that is byte-identical to real. The other two
candidate mechanisms in the entry — a seed, or a selection override — are
allowed only with S0 evidence that they reproduce real's observable output
(the r25 shape and the 15 slotop cells, re-derived by a fresh probe — the
g211 report is background, not evidence for this slice).
